# Opening Garrick + Marlow Interaction

## 1. Player Appears in Garrick's Inn

Character creation ends.

The player appears inside the common room and receives control immediately.

There is no forced conversation and no objective saying:

**Talk to Garrick.**

Garrick notices the unfamiliar face, but gives the player enough room to act first.

Whatever the player does becomes the way Garrick first engages them.

---

# REACTIVE FIRST CONTACT

## Player Approaches Garrick Normally

As the player approaches the bar:

**Garrick:** "New face."

He gives them a quick look over.

**Garrick:** "Garrick. I own the place."

**Garrick:** "You got a name?"

**Player:** "[NAME]."

**Garrick:** "[NAME]. Right."

Continue to **Shared Garrick Conversation**.

---

## Player Tries the Kitchen

Before the player can wander into the working kitchen:

**Garrick:** "Oi."

Player turns.

**Garrick:** "Don't even know your name and you're already trying to get into my kitchen?"

Possible responses:

**Player:** "Just looking around."

**Garrick:** "Look from this side."

Or:

**Player:** "Your kitchen?"

**Garrick:** "Technically."

Or:

**Player:** "Who's going to stop me?"

Garrick just looks at them.

**Garrick:** "You really need that answered?"

Then:

**Garrick:** "Garrick. I own the place."

Continue into introduction.

Sylvie is NOT introduced yet.

---

## Player Goes Upstairs

As they start toward the rooms:

**Garrick:** "Rooms aren't free."

Possible responses:

### "How much?"

Garrick gives the actual room price.

The cheapest room is deliberately around **5–10 coins more than the player's starting wealth**.

The player now understands that earning money has immediate value.

### "I was just looking."

**Garrick:** "Then you've taken a real interest in stairs."

### "I don't have enough."

Garrick looks them over.

**Garrick:** "Figured."

Potentially:

**Garrick:** "Might be able to fix that."

**Player:** "The money?"

**Garrick:** "Your situation."

Then:

**Garrick:** "Garrick. Place is mine."

Continue into shared conversation.

Do NOT explain the full Rest Quality system yet.

The player only needs to understand:

**Proper room/bed = useful and costs money.**

---

## Player Tries Marlow's Basement Door

Marlow is sitting at a nearby table eating.

The player tries the laboratory door.

**Marlow:** "Um... excuse me."

Player looks toward him.

**Marlow:** "That's my laboratory."

Small pause.

**Marlow:** "You're trying to enter it."

If the player tries again:

**Marlow:** "Garrick?"

Without much urgency:

**Garrick:** "I see 'em, Bottle-Brain."

Garrick addresses the player.

**Garrick:** "You always introduce yourself by trying locked doors?"

Possible:

**Player:** "I didn't know it was his."

**Garrick:** "That's usually what the lock's for."

Then:

**Garrick:** "Garrick. I own the place."

Marlow returns to his meal for now.

The player has heard **Bottle-Brain**, but Marlow has not necessarily introduced his real name yet.

---

## Player Investigates the Hunting Board

The player is allowed to READ it.

Reading is not a crime.

Trying to actually take a posting gets Garrick involved.

**Garrick:** "Looking for work?"

Possible:

**Player:** "What's this?"

**Garrick:** "People have problems. They pay somebody else to make 'em stop."

Or:

**Player:** "I'll take this one."

**Garrick:** "No."

**Player:** "Why not?"

Garrick looks them over.

**Garrick:** "Because I don't know you."

He looks toward the posting.

**Garrick:** "Don't know if you can handle what's on the other end of that, either."

Then the important moral reason:

**Garrick:** "I'm not sending some fresh face out there just to find out what eats 'em."

Garrick isn't protecting game progression.

He's refusing to send an unknown stranger to their death.

Then introduction.

---

## Player Touches Garrick's Merchandise

Looking at merchandise is completely allowed.

Trying to take it without paying is different.

Player reaches for a weapon.

**Garrick:** "I'd leave that where it is."

Possible:

**Player:** "Is it for sale?"

**Garrick:** "Now you're asking the right question."

Or:

**Player:** "What happens if I don't?"

Garrick looks at them.

**Garrick:** "You planning on finding out?"

For alcohol:

Player attempts to take a bottle.

**Garrick:** "That's an interesting way to order a drink."

This transitions naturally into Garrick's merchant/owner introduction.

---

## Player Talks to Marlow First

Marlow looks up from his meal.

**Marlow:** "Hello."

He puts down what he's eating.

**Marlow:** "Sorry. Were you looking for someone?"

Garrick notices.

**Garrick:** "Hm. New face and already talking to Bottle-Brain."

Marlow looks toward him.

**Marlow:** "I can hear you."

**Garrick:** "That's why I said it."

Marlow looks back toward the player.

**Marlow:** "He does this."

Then Garrick introduces himself.

This route means the player has interacted with Marlow before the shared scene, but Marlow's proper role is still unrevealed.

---

## Player Sits and Does Nothing

Allowed.

No tutorial timer immediately attacks them.

The inn continues functioning.

Marlow eats.

Garrick works.

Eventually Garrick calls over:

**Garrick:** "You know there aren't any maids here."

Player looks toward him.

**Garrick:** "You want a drink, you come to the bar."

If player approaches:

**Garrick:** "Garrick. I own the inn."

If they continue sitting, Garrick lets them.

---

## Player Immediately Leaves

Player approaches the front door.

Garrick notices.

**Garrick:** "Didn't even make it to the bar."

If the player turns/responds, this can lead into Garrick's introduction.

If they continue:

**The door opens.**

They leave.

No invisible wall.

No "Complete Tutorial First."

When they eventually return, Garrick remembers.

Possible:

**Garrick:** "Back already?"

Or:

**Garrick:** "Look who survived."

Then introduction can occur.

---

# AGGRESSIVE / THEFT BRANCH

If the player repeatedly attempts to steal after Garrick warns them, they receive multiple opportunities to stop.

Example:

**Garrick:** "Put it back."

Player refuses.

Garrick gives them another chance.

**Garrick:** "Kid."

Beat.

**Garrick:** "Put it back."

If they explicitly continue:

Garrick comes around the bar.

Combat begins.

This is an optional sequence-break first combat.

Garrick fights nonlethally with fists/physical force.

He is extraordinarily difficult at starting power but mechanically beatable.

## Player Loses

Garrick ends the fight without killing them.

Potentially offers them a hand up.

**Garrick:** "There."

**Garrick:** "Now that we've learned some manners..."

**Garrick:** "How about a proper introduction?"

Normal opening resumes.

## Player Somehow Beats Garrick

Garrick accepts the loss.

**Garrick:** "Well."

He gets back up.

**Garrick:** "I'm man enough to know when I've been beat."

But:

**Garrick:** "Still can't let you steal from me."

He offers peace, potentially including a drink.

Importantly:

### Hunting board becomes available early.

Garrick's original objection was that he didn't know whether the player could survive.

That question has now been answered rather dramatically.

If the player continues stealing anyway, town-watch escalation occurs according to the previously approved consequence branch.

---

# SHARED GARRICK CONVERSATION

Once Garrick has naturally engaged the player, the branches converge.

Garrick learns their name if he hasn't already.

**Garrick:** "You got a name?"

**Player:** "[NAME]."

**Garrick:** "[NAME]. Right."

From here Garrick establishes only information the player has NOT already learned.

Broadly:

- he owns the inn
- he sells drinks
- he sells basic weapons and armor
- rooms are available upstairs
- work passes through the board

Do NOT have Garrick list these like game features.

Conversation adapts.

If the player already learned rooms cost money, Garrick doesn't repeat it.

If they inspected the board, he can refer back to that.

If they tried stealing a sword, they clearly already know he sells equipment.

---

# MARLOW'S ACCIDENT

During the Garrick interaction:

**CRASH.**

Glass breaks at Marlow's table.

Marlow stares at what he has dropped.

This is NOT meaningless slapstick.

He has broken the **last usable sample connected to the treatment he's developing for his sick troll**.

Garrick looks over.

**Garrick:** "Bottle-Brain."

Marlow examines the damage.

**Marlow:** "...That's unfortunate."

Garrick knows Marlow well enough to recognize the understatement.

**Garrick:** "How unfortunate?"

Marlow carefully gathers what's salvageable.

**Marlow:** "That was the last usable sample I had."

Garrick immediately understands what this concerns.

**Garrick:** "Thought you already put in a request."

**Marlow:** "I did."

**Garrick:** "Watch still sitting on it?"

**Marlow:** "They have more urgent matters."

Garrick knows exactly what that means.

He looks at the player.

Then back at Marlow.

**Garrick:** "Might've found you another pair of legs."

Marlow follows his gaze.

**Marlow:** "You don't know them."

**Garrick:** "Nope."

If the player has expressed interest in work:

**Garrick:** "But they're looking for work."

If they haven't:

**Garrick:** "But they're standing right here."

Marlow looks between Garrick and the player.

**Marlow:** "That's not usually how I choose expedition partners."

**Garrick:** "Good thing you're not going."

Marlow doesn't look convinced.

Garrick gestures toward the player.

**Garrick:** "Look, they're not much."

Player can visibly react.

**Garrick:** "But they're what you've got."

**Marlow:** "Garrick..."

Garrick becomes slightly more serious.

**Garrick:** "Bottle-Brain, you're running out of time."

That lands.

Marlow looks at the broken sample.

He knows Garrick is right.

---

# MARLOW DECIDES TO SHOW THE PLAYER

Marlow looks at the player properly now.

**Marlow:** "If you're actually considering this... you should see what I'm working on first."

**Player:** "Where?"

Marlow indicates the basement.

**Marlow:** "Downstairs. My laboratory."

If the player previously tried entering:

**Player:** "The room you wouldn't let me into?"

**Marlow:** "Yes."

Pause.

**Marlow:** "Under slightly different circumstances."

Now the invitation downstairs has been explicitly established.

Only NOW does Garrick give the warning:

**Garrick:** "They try anything funny down there, you holler."

Garrick looks directly at the player.

**Garrick:** "Then there's a big boot coming for their face."

**Player:** "Mine?"

**Garrick:** "You see anybody else I just threatened?"

Then Garrick gestures toward Marlow.

**Garrick:** "You wanted work."

Beat.

**Garrick:** "There's work."

---

# PLAYER RESPONSE

The player can respond:

### "What does he need?"

Marlow:

**Marlow:** "A few things I can't leave to collect myself."

He doesn't explain the troll publicly.

**Marlow:** "It'll make more sense when you see why."

### "How much does it pay?"

Marlow gives the current quest payment.

He is not offended.

This is work. Asking about payment is reasonable.

One possible character beat we liked was Garrick ensuring Marlow doesn't underpay:

**Marlow:** "I can pay you [amount]."

**Garrick:** "[slightly higher amount]."

Marlow looks at him.

**Marlow:** "Garrick."

**Garrick:** "You want it done today."

Exact payment is not finalized.

### "Sure."

Marlow may be slightly surprised by the immediate agreement.

**Marlow:** "You don't know what I need yet."

The player can respond according to characterization.

Possible:

**Player:** "You said you're running out of time."

Or:

**Player:** "Work's work."

### "No thanks."

Marlow does not beg.

**Marlow:** "All right."

Garrick does not shame the player.

**Garrick:** "Your call."

The world continues.

The player can reconsider while there is still time.

If they don't, the troll eventually dies and the established Marlow departure branch occurs.

---

# PLAYER AGREES TO HEAR MARLOW OUT

Marlow gathers:

- the surviving research material
- his notes/belongings from the table
- whatever else he needs

He walks toward the basement door.

This is the same door that was previously socially/private-access restricted.

Marlow unlocks it.

If appropriate, he can look toward the player.

**Marlow:** "Come on, then."

The player physically follows him downstairs.

No teleport.

No fade directly into a dialogue scene.

---

# END OF GARRICK/MARLOW OPENING INTERACTION

At this point:

- Garrick has been established as the player's first social anchor.
- Garrick knows the player's name.
- The player understands the inn has rooms, commerce and work without receiving a giant mechanics dump.
- Bottle-Brain has been established as Garrick's normal nickname for Marlow.
- Marlow has introduced himself naturally if needed.
- Garrick and Marlow already feel like people with history together.
- Marlow has a real urgent problem.
- Garrick has NOT pretended to trust or endorse a stranger he just met.
- Marlow has NOT casually shown his vulnerable pet/lab to a random stranger.
- The player has been offered an opportunity rather than assigned Tutorial Quest #1.
- The player may genuinely refuse.
- The basement invitation has been explicitly established before Garrick's boot warning.
- Marlow now leads the player into the laboratory, where the next scene begins.

From this point onward, Marlow relocates to his laboratory as his normal inn location.
