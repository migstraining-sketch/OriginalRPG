# Characters

## Garrick

**Role:** Innkeeper, bartender, early merchant, room landlord, contract-board gatekeeper, first social anchor.

**Core personality:** Rugged, large, strong, blunt, practical, funny without trying too hard, casually intimidating, protective beneath the rough exterior. Inspiration reference only: Bobby Singer-type gruff competence and warmth.

**Early functions:**
- welcomes the new player
- sells basic weapons and armor
- rents rooms upstairs
- controls access to the contract board
- reacts contextually if the player trespasses, steals, idles, tries to leave, or provokes him
- uses affectionate insults for Marlow, with **Bottle-Brain** as the current working recurring nickname

**Relationship with Marlow:** Officially their arrangement is practical. Marlow provides excellent ale and useful medicinal/alchemical expertise; Garrick provides the basement laboratory/home. Unofficially they are effectively brothers. Do not state this outright early. Let the player infer it.

**Key behavioral signal:** If Marlow's troll dies and Marlow leaves, Garrick says only **"Marlow left."** Using Marlow's real name instead of Bottle-Brain signals how serious the moment is.

**Established line into Sylvie:**

> "Take it through to her. I'd call her out, but I've survived this long by remembering one thing."
>
> "My inn. Her kitchen."

## Marlow

**Role:** Alchemist, naturalist, healer, expeditionary researcher, owner/user of the inn's basement laboratory, Potion Making mentor.

**Core personality:** Gentle, intelligent, slightly awkward, easily distracted, curious, earnest, creature-focused, and highly competent in his field. Inspiration reference only: Newt Scamander-like quiet creature curiosity.

**Important distinction:** Marlow is not a bumbling scientist. His upstairs accident is personality texture, not evidence that he is incompetent.

**Opening location:** Initially sitting in the common room with food/drink because he is taking a meal/break. He may still have notes, herbs, specimens, bottles, or a satchel with him.

**Opening quest:** Marlow rescued a baby troll from drowning. The troll is normally green but becomes pale, loses appetite, and loses much of its regeneration. Marlow is researching treatment but will not leave the deteriorating creature unattended.

Preferred core explanation:

> **"He was drowning."**

**Potion Making:** Marlow teaches and unlocks Potion Making. The first recipe is Health Potion.

**Relationship with Garrick:** Marlow accidentally invented extremely good ale during fermentation research. He barely drinks it himself. He supplies Garrick's inn; Garrick supplies his home/lab.

## Sylvie

**Role:** Garrick's inn chef and Cooking mentor.

**Core personality:** Exceptionally skilled, fully confident, perfectionist, intensely territorial about her kitchen, passionate about food, hates wasting edible ingredients, cares through feeding people rather than speeches. Inspiration reference only: Sanji-like culinary intensity and standards translated into an original character.

**Core contradiction:**

**If you're hungry and broke, Sylvie will feed you.**

**If you're an inexperienced stranger asking to touch her knives and stove, that is an entirely different conversation.**

**Expertise:** Sylvie has encyclopedic practical knowledge of edible creatures and ingredients. She can often identify species, freshness, preservation, diet, anatomy, and preparation implications from physical evidence. This complements Marlow rather than duplicating him.

Example distinction:

> **Marlow:** "The muscle fiber suggests a semi-aquatic diet and frequent bursts of movement."
>
> **Sylvie:** "It swims."

**First kitchen entrance:**

> **Sylvie:** "Out."
>
> **Player:** "Garrick sent me."

Her attitude changes when she notices the edible material.

**Cooking philosophy:** Respect ingredients, understand their condition, and use appropriate technique. Recipe rarity does not equal quality.

**Teaching arrangement:** Player brings enough raw ingredient to practice plus an additional useful pantry contribution. Early on she does not trust novice cooked food enough to serve it to customers.

Preferred pantry explanation:

> "Bring enough to cook with."
>
> "And enough that I'm not teaching you at everyone else's expense."

If asked about feeding people who cannot pay:

> **"They're hungry, aren't they?"**

**Cooking unlock:** Sylvie demonstrates a dish → player tastes → player asks to learn → Sylvie explains arrangement → player accepts → **Cooking unlocks**.

Preferred follow-up:

> **"Don't look so pleased. You haven't cooked anything yet."**

First personal Cooking attempt is optional immediately afterward.

**Long-term behavioral progression:**
- Beginner: "Out."
- Trusted: potentially "Need the stove?"

The behavioral shift itself should communicate relationship progression.
