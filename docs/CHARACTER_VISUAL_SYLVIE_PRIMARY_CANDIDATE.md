## Focused refinement brief

### Footwear

Preserve closed, sturdy footwear and strong silhouette readability, but simplify the current boots away from fantasy-adventurer styling.

Target practical kitchen/work boots with:
- reliable grip
- toe/foot protection
- ankle stability
- easy movement on hard or potentially wet kitchen floors
- minimal decorative straps, armor-like segmentation, or travel-adventurer hardware

### Gloves

Do **not** establish fingerless or black gloves as Sylvie's default food-preparation state.

For ordinary knife work, seasoning, plating, ingredient handling, and most food preparation, show **clean bare hands**.

Task-specific gloves remain plausible for:
- ovens
- hot pans
- heavy handling
- cleaning
- maintenance

They are equipment for a task, not a permanent silhouette feature.

### Equipment clutter

Reduce remaining belt/pouch hardware.

Visible priority order:
1. Sylvie herself and the apron silhouette
2. chef knife
3. towel/cloth
4. at most one or two genuinely useful secondary attachments

Do not give her an adventurer utility belt in the kitchen.

### Outfit construction

Use the current turnaround as the strongest construction reference.

Preserve:
- white practical working layer
- dark apron
- loose dark trousers
- wine-red sash
- free arm/shoulder movement
- professional coverage appropriate to a hot active kitchen

Keep tailoring attractive and controlled without fanservice-first construction.

### Ornament

Keep decorative patterning restrained.

The red accent and subtle apron detail are already enough to carry designed fantasy polish. Craftsmanship, fit, cleanliness, material quality, posture, and tool handling should communicate Sylvie's authority more strongly than embroidery.
