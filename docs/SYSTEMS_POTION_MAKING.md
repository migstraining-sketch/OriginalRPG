# Opening Potion Making

Potion Making is introduced through Marlow's sick-troll quest and is the first major profession/system unlock in the standard opening.

## Narrative reason

Marlow's rescued baby troll has become pale, stopped eating, and lost much of its natural regeneration. Marlow has been researching a treatment but cannot leave the deteriorating creature unattended.

The player gathers:
- Bloodleaf
- Silvermoss
- Mooncalf Milk

## First potion interaction

Back in Marlow's laboratory, the player physically performs the treatment preparation rather than pressing a generic Craft button:

**Prepare Bloodleaf → prepare Silvermoss → measure Mooncalf Milk → combine in correct sequence → control heat/stirring → finish experimental Health Potion**

Marlow supervises and prevents catastrophic first-attempt errors.

The first dose goes to the troll. Successful treatment is communicated physically:
- color begins returning
- most importantly, appetite returns

Preferred beat:

> **Marlow:** "It worked."

Player receives the remaining Health Potion.

## Unlock

**Potion Making unlocked.**  
**Health Potion recipe learned.**

Do not dump Mana, Strength, or future recipes into a giant recipe menu at this moment. The player learns exactly one recipe and why it matters.

## Lab access

Marlow's lab remains the initial Potion Making location.

Continued access should operate through contribution of useful materials/specimens/animal feed/lab supplies rather than a generic flat crafting fee.

Exact contribution ratios/economy remain unresolved.

## Current role separation

- Potions handle immediate/reactive effects such as healing.
- Food does **not** directly heal.
- Cooking and Potion Making should remain distinct systems even though they both transform ingredients.
