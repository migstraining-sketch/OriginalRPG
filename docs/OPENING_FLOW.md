# Opening Flow

**Target:** roughly 20–30 minutes for a player following the intended path, with healthy variance around **23–35 minutes** based on exploration, dialogue reading, combat speed, and contract choice.

The pacing rule is that the opening never actively teaches more than **one new mental model at a time**.

## 0:00–1:30 — Character Creation → Arrival

Character creation ends and the player loads directly into Garrick's common room.

No exposition movie. No quest objective yet. Player has control immediately.

Visible but unexplained promises:
- Garrick behind bar → social anchor / merchant
- contract board → work
- upstairs → rooms
- kitchen door → private inn space
- Marlow eating near basement door → another inhabitant
- basement door → restricted/private area
- front door → outside world

## 1:30–4:00 — Reactive Garrick Introduction

There is one narrative destination but several entrances.

### Normal approach
Garrick notices the new face, introduces himself, asks the player's name, and the shared conversation begins.

### Upstairs
Garrick explains rooms are not free. This introduces room rental and can naturally point toward work.

### Kitchen
Garrick stops a stranger from entering a private working kitchen. He does not explain Sylvie yet.

### Basement
Marlow sees the player trying his laboratory door and objects. Garrick joins in. This transitions into Garrick's introduction rather than creating an infinite blocked-action bark.

### Contract board
The player may inspect enough to know jobs exist. Trying to take one causes Garrick to intervene because he does not yet know whether the stranger can handle themselves.

### Merchandise
Looking is allowed. Taking without paying gets a warning and Garrick introduction. Repeated escalation can move into criminal/combat branches.

### Marlow first
The player may talk to Marlow. Garrick notices the new face already speaking with Bottle-Brain and enters naturally.

### Idling
Allowed. After a while Garrick can acknowledge them and invite interaction.

### Leaving
Garrick gives one contextual remark. If the player continues, they actually leave. No invisible wall. The opening remains available when they return, and Garrick remembers that they walked straight back out.

All ordinary branches converge into Garrick's conversation, adapting to what the player has already discovered.

## ~3:00–5:00 — Bottle-Brain Accident

During Garrick's interaction, Marlow breaks the last usable sample connected to urgent research.

Garrick asks about the town-watch request. Marlow explains the watch still has more urgent human matters.

Preferred beat:

> **Garrick:** "Might've found you another pair of legs."

Marlow objects because Garrick does not know the player.

The player is not being declared trustworthy or heroic. They are simply present, mobile, and possibly looking for work.

Marlow eventually invites the player downstairs. Only after the invitation does Garrick warn Marlow to holler if the player tries anything funny.

The player may decline. Nothing traps them.

## 5:00–7:00 — First Laboratory Visit

Marlow unlocks the basement. Player follows downstairs.

Control returns briefly so the player can inspect the lab before another exposition-heavy conversation.

Environmental storytelling:
- expedition equipment
- handwritten notes
- creature sketches
- specimens
- alchemy tools
- plants
- habitats
- evidence of extensive travel

The Garrick/Marlow ale arrangement may be established naturally here.

## 7:00–9:00 — Sick Troll Reveal

Marlow's baby troll is introduced as a creature he genuinely loves, not a quest prop.

The player learns:
- Marlow found it drowning during an expedition
- preferred explanation: **"He was drowning."**
- healthy coloration should be green
- it has become pale
- appetite disappeared
- regeneration deteriorated
- Marlow has been researching treatment
- time matters

Marlow would normally gather supplies himself but will not leave this creature while it is deteriorating.

## Quest Offer

Marlow needs:
- Bloodleaf
- Silvermoss
- Mooncalf Milk

Objective can be recorded simply as **Help Marlow prepare a treatment** with components listed.

Marlow shows/describes what Bloodleaf looks like, where Silvermoss grows, and how he would peacefully obtain Mooncalf Milk.

No Taming tutorial or skill unlock occurs.

## 9:00–14:00 — Woodland Expedition

Player physically leaves the inn and travels into nearby woodland. Include a brief stretch of calm travel before danger.

### First combat
Ordinary woodland wildlife provides likely first hostile encounter. Combat instruction appears only when combat begins and should be minimal/contextual.

### Bloodleaf
Player recognizes it from Marlow's description and harvests useful portions without uprooting the whole plant.

### Silvermoss
Found by observing damp shaded stone rather than following a glowing trail.

### Mooncalf Milk
Player can follow Marlow's peaceful approach and gain cooperation. They may also frighten, attack, chase, or kill the Mooncalf if they insist. The quest continues if the ingredient is logically obtained. Marlow can react later.

## 14:00–16:00 — Mossback Miniboss

Marlow has already established Mossbacks are normally docile if given space.

The player encounters one, gives it appropriate space, and it continues to pursue and attack.

No corruption visuals, villain clue, Evil Shard, or dramatic explanation.

After the fight, the behavior remains unexplained.

## 16:00–18:00 — Return to Marlow

Player physically returns to the inn/lab.

Marlow asks naturalist questions: was it cornered, injured, defending young, provoked?

When ordinary explanations fail:

> **Marlow:** "That's strange."

He intends to investigate. No main-story quest appears.

## 18:00–20:00 — First Potion Making

Under Marlow's supervision, player physically performs:

**Prepare Bloodleaf → prepare Silvermoss → measure Mooncalf Milk → combine correctly → control heat/stirring → finish mixture**

Marlow prevents catastrophic first-attempt errors.

First dose goes to the troll.

Color begins returning. Most importantly, it becomes hungry again.

> **Marlow:** "It worked."

Player receives remaining Health Potion.

**Potion Making learned.**

**Health Potion recipe learned.**

No giant recipe catalogue appears.

Marlow explains future lab use requires useful contributions rather than a flat generic crafting fee.

## 20:00–21:00 — Garrick Recognizes the Player

Player returns/emerges to Garrick.

Garrick hears the treatment worked and responds simply, likely **"Good."**

He now has evidence the player can navigate the woods, handle combat, return alive, and deal with unexpected trouble.

The contract board opens.

If the player previously defeated Garrick, treat this as already sequence-broken rather than a new unlock.

## Room Rental Hook

Room rental is available Day One but not mandatory.

Cheapest room costs roughly **5–10 coins beyond starting wealth**. Marlow's reward moves the player closer. Exploration may make it affordable already; if so, let them rent it.

No Rest Quality tutorial yet.

## 21:00–22:00 — First Hunting Board Choice

All three starter contracts appear with equal visual weight. No highlighted main quest, no gold posting, no difficulty ordering.

Visible problems:
- Mud in the Moonrice
- Three Missing by Morning
- When the Wheel Stopped

Each communicates client, location, problem, and reward, not the solution.

Other two remain available after choosing one.

## 22:00–27:00ish — First Hunting Contract

Each targets roughly 5–8 minutes on first play and uses the Hunting grammar:

**Inspect → Interpret → Follow → Act → Harvest**

See `CONTRACTS.md` for full details.

Completing any one formally unlocks **Hunting** after the wildlife problem is actually resolved, not merely after the first kill.

## ~27:00–29:00 — Return to Garrick

Player returns with outcome and edible material.

Garrick acknowledges completed work without a graduation ceremony.

Then he notices the ingredient and sends the player to Sylvie.

Established beat:

> **Garrick:** "Take it through to her. I'd call her out, but I've survived this long by remembering one thing."
>
> **Garrick:** "My inn. Her kitchen."

The same kitchen that was socially inaccessible earlier is now somewhere Garrick explicitly instructs the player to enter.

## 29:00–32:00 — Sylvie Introduction

Player enters.

> **Sylvie:** "Out."
>
> **Player:** "Garrick sent me."

She notices the ingredient and identifies it/its condition through culinary expertise.

Possible input states:
- Fresh Reedback Haunch
- Preserved Reedback Cut
- Fresh Duskhen Eggs
- Fresh Brookmaw Tail
- Naturally Shed Brookmaw Tail

Sylvie is not clairvoyant. For Nightquill resolution, player must tell her what happened.

## Sylvie Demonstration

Sylvie prepares a simple dish through the same Cooking system the player later uses:

**Inspect → Prepare → Set Up → Cook → Read → Remove → Finish**

Different ingredients demonstrate different principles without separate minigames.

All resulting dishes apply the placeholder **Well Fed** state. Food does not heal directly.

## Taste

Preferred beat:

> **Player:** "...What did you do to this?"
>
> **Sylvie:** "Cooked it."
>
> **Player:** "No. Seriously."
>
> **Sylvie:** "So am I."

## ~32:00 — Cooking Offer and Unlock

Player asks to learn.

Sylvie requires enough ingredient to practice plus useful pantry contribution.

Preferred explanation:

> "Bring enough to cook with."
>
> "And enough that I'm not teaching you at everyone else's expense."

If asked about feeding someone who cannot pay:

> **"They're hungry, aren't they?"**

Player accepts.

**Cooking learned.**

Sylvie may add:

> **"Don't look so pleased. You haven't cooked anything yet."**

First supervised personal Cooking attempt is optional. The opening/tutorial phase is considered complete here.

## Mandatory vs optional

Mandatory for standard route:
- functional Garrick engagement unless leaving/sequence-breaking
- Mossback fight on Marlow route
- first Health Potion craft if completing Marlow route
- one starter Hunting contract to reach Hunting/Sylvie opening
- Sylvie demonstration and pantry agreement to unlock Cooking

Optional:
- explore every inn area
- rent room immediately
- peaceful Mooncalf interaction
- kill Hunting target
- complete all three starter contracts
- first personal Cooking attempt

## Major sequence breaks

- Player immediately leaves: allowed.
- Player steals/provokes Garrick: warnings/escalation/combat branch.
- Player somehow defeats Garrick: Hunting-board access can occur early.
- Player reaches Hunting before Marlow: allowed; possible order becomes Hunting → Sylvie/Cooking → Marlow/Potion Making later.
- Player refuses Marlow: allowed; troll remains on real clock.
- Troll dies if ignored too long; Marlow leaves for a significant period.
- Player kills Mooncalf: allowed if ingredient obtained logically; Marlow can react.
- Non-lethal Hunting resolutions fully supported.

## Opening endpoint

At the end, player potentially has:
- Potion Making
- Hunting
- Cooking

And clear physical homes for each:
- Marlow's lab
- Garrick's board / wilderness
- Sylvie's kitchen

Two starter contracts remain, Garrick sells gear, a room waits upstairs, Health Potion ingredients can be gathered again, Sylvie can supervise Cooking, outside world is open, and the unexplained Mossback behavior quietly remains in the background.
